About Global Social Hub

Curiosity · Context · Discovery

Global Social Hub is an independent digital publication that helps readers keep up with the discoveries shaping our world. We cover space exploration, science, new discoveries, artificial intelligence, technology, and important world news — and we present them in a clear, readable way, always pointing readers to the original reporting.

What We Cover

  • Space — missions, telescopes, planets, and the people pushing exploration forward.
  • Science — research and findings from universities, laboratories, and journals.
  • Discoveries — archaeology, paleontology, oceans, and the natural world.
  • AI & Technology — artificial intelligence, computing, and the companies building them.
  • World News — significant global developments with context.

How We Work

Our editors monitor trusted primary sources — including space agencies such as NASA and ESA, scientific publishers, research institutions, and established news organizations such as ScienceDaily, TechCrunch, and Phys.org. For each story, we publish a headline and a short summary in our own words, together with a clear link so you can read the full original report at its source. Our story feed is refreshed several times a day so readers see recent developments first.

Our Standards

  • Credit: original reporting belongs to its publishers; we always link to the source.
  • Accuracy: we summarize carefully and correct mistakes when we find them. Spotted an error? Please tell us.
  • Clarity: no hype for its own sake — strong stories, honestly told.

Who We Are

Global Social Hub is produced by a small independent team of writers and editors who love science and exploration. The site works alongside our social media community, where the same stories are shared as short videos and posts for discussion. Thank you for reading — we are glad you are here.
